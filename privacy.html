Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information riots.wtf collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using the Site and Services you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

  • Discord account data: when you sign in with Discord, we receive your Discord ID, username, display name and avatar to identify your account and link your license keys.
  • License & usage data: your keys, activation status, HWID (hardware identifier), and basic usage counts needed to operate and protect the Services.
  • Support data: messages you send us through tickets or Discord.
  • Payment data: handled by our processor. We receive order and delivery status, not your full card details.

2. How we use it

  • To deliver, activate and manage your purchases and license keys.
  • To provide support and respond to your requests.
  • To detect fraud, abuse, key sharing and to enforce our Terms of Service.
  • To improve the Site and our products.

3. Third-party services

We rely on trusted providers to run the Services, including Komerza for payments, Discord for login, and our licensing provider for key management. These providers process data under their own privacy policies.

4. Cookies & local storage

We use a session token and browser local storage to keep you logged in and remember your cart and preferences. We do not sell your data or use invasive third-party ad tracking.

5. Data retention

We keep account and license data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Services, resolve disputes and enforce our agreements. You can request deletion of your data (subject to records we must keep for legal or fraud-prevention reasons).

6. Your choices

  • Log out at any time from your dashboard.
  • Request access to or deletion of your data by opening a ticket or contacting us on Discord.
  • Revoke Discord authorization from your Discord account settings.

7. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure. Keep your key and account credentials private.

8. Children

The Services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

9. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above reflects the current version.

10. Contact

Questions about your privacy? Reach us on Discord or open a ticket from your dashboard.
